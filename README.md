# muse-pastself-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [That Quiet Sunday in March](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca).

## The entry

> When Dad offers to teach you his accounts spreadsheet on that quiet Sunday in March, sit down - it is the last time he will ever ask.
Because you said you were busy, he never asked again, and ten years later you would trade every busy Sunday since for that one hour back.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: Names one concrete, picturable moment (a quiet Sunday in March, a specific offer) and the second line traces real cause and effect (said busy -> never asked again -> ten years of regret) instead of restating the warning.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 e4fc327e870cd87107c449b2c842beb9c3f222018b3d5c76f066c3a0267d36e1).
